Accessibility by design

Inclusive learning starts with the authoring experience.

We're designing CourseLab around WCAG 2.2 AA and accessible authoring practices: keyboard operation, clear structure, readable contrast, and prompts for alternative text. Accessibility checks and manual testing will grow with the product.

This prototype has not been independently audited or certified as WCAG conformant.
