Educational Philosophies and Homeschool

Install the 12 extracted Rise lessons as a new CourseLab course. This uses CourseLab's native enrollment codes, learner progress, and gradebook rather than the expired Rise player.

Before installing

Sign in as the instructor who should own the course. The course will be created under the currently signed-in instructor account, not automatically assigned to another person. Installing again creates another copy.

15 instructional videos will show placeholders until their unlisted YouTube URLs are added to imported/educational-philosophies/video-urls.json in GitHub. Learners can complete lessons without those links; do not enroll students until videos are ready.

Instructor gradebook currently tracks completed lessons, not quiz scores or survey responses. Assessment is practice scoring; survey responses stay in the browser session and are not submitted.

Important: This is a converted presentation, not a pixel-identical Rise player. Verify the course and quizzes before publishing for students.
